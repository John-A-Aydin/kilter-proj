About this experiment

Loading trained transformer…

Your picks are given to the model exactly, and it was trained to complete climbs from such picks. Decoding only rules out what no training climb does: repeating a hold, or having other than one or two start and finish holds. Generated climbs have not been checked for climbability.

Board visualization uses Boardsesh artwork, traced hold shapes, and its Aura renderer (Apache 2.0 license). Inference uses ONNX Runtime Web.
